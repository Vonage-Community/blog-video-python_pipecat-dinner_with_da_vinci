      Where your audio goes

      Your microphone audio travels through Vonage Video to the agent and on to Deepgram. The text of the conversation goes to OpenAI, and his replies to ElevenLabs, for the length of the call. His voice reaches you through the Vonage Video session, never directly from a provider. Nothing is stored after the call.
